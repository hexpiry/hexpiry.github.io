Add in seconds

Scan a document, done.

Point your camera at a passport, licence or card and Hexpiry fills in the details for you — type, expiry date, country and reference number. Or add anything by hand in a few taps.

Scanning happens entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Everything that expires

Passports to rego, all sorted.

Browse everything by type — each with its own picture, so you can spot it at a glance:

  • 🛂 Passports, with each country's flag
  • 🪪 Driver licences and 🚗 vehicle registration
  • 💳 Bank cards and ☂️ insurance policies
  • 📜 Certifications, 🔁 subscriptions and 🏷️ anything else

Never get caught out

Reminders that arrive in time.

Set how far ahead you want the heads-up and Hexpiry does the rest:

  • Each type starts with a sensible lead time — passports months ahead (many countries need months of validity to let you in), subscriptions a week before they renew.
  • Repeat reminders so an important renewal doesn't slip.
  • A colour-coded dashboard: red for expired, orange for soon, green for plenty of time.
  • Your expired count, right on the app icon.

For the whole household

Everyone's documents, one tidy list.

Add the people in your household and tag each item to whoever it belongs to. Keep the family's passports, licences and cards organised in a single place.

At a glance

Right on your home screen.

Add the Hexpiry widget and see what's expiring next without opening the app. It refreshes each day so the countdown is always current.

Private by design.

Hexpiry has no server of its own. Your items live only on your devices and in your own private iCloud — the developer never sees your data. No account to create, no tracking, no ads.

Hexpiry tracks expiry, not secrets: it only needs a label and a date, never full card numbers.
